Privacy Policy for DentalHistory

DentalHistory is an offline-first app for tracking personal dental history, treatments, doctors, reminders, and related expenses.

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DentalHistory handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to work locally on your device. It does not require an account, does not provide cloud sync, and does not intentionally send your dental records to the developer.

2. Information stored by the app

DentalHistory may store information that you enter directly into the app, including:

  • tooth records and treatment history,
  • doctor names and treatment types,
  • appointment and reminder details,
  • treatment costs and personal notes,
  • optional attachments such as photos, X-rays, or PDF files, if you choose to add them.

This information is stored locally on your device.

3. How your data is used

Your data is used only to provide the app's features, such as viewing treatment history, tracking reminders, managing expenses, and restoring backups.

4. Data sharing and transmission

DentalHistory does not sell your personal data. The app does not require user registration and does not intentionally transmit your dental records to the developer's servers.

Data may leave your device only when you explicitly choose actions such as exporting a backup, sharing a file, or opening files through your device's operating system or third-party apps. In those cases, handling of the exported file depends on the services or apps you choose.

5. Backups and exported files

If you use the backup or export features, the app creates a JSON backup file containing the data needed to restore your records. This file is created only at your request.

You are responsible for where you store, send, or upload exported backup files. If you place them in cloud storage, email, messaging apps, or third-party services, their privacy practices will apply.

6. Permissions and system features

Depending on your device and the features you use, DentalHistory may access system file pickers, local notifications, or sharing features in order to:

  • import a backup file,
  • export or share a backup file,
  • schedule reminder notifications,
  • attach files you intentionally select.

The app does not use these features to collect data beyond what is necessary for the requested action.

7. Third-party services

DentalHistory does not provide cloud accounts, in-app social features, or advertising-based tracking. However, the app may rely on operating-system components or standard mobile platform services, such as Android or Google Play, for app distribution, crash delivery handled by the platform, notifications, file access, or installation.

8. Data security

Because your records are stored locally, the security of your data depends in part on the security of your device and any services you use to store exported files. You should protect your device, keep backups in safe locations, and avoid sharing exported files unless necessary.

9. Children's privacy

DentalHistory is not specifically directed to children. If the app is used to store records relating to another person, including a child, the user is responsible for ensuring they have the appropriate authority to manage that information.

10. Data retention and deletion

All data you enter into DentalHistory is stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you choose to delete it. No copy is kept by the developer.

You can delete your data at any time in the following ways:

  • Delete individual records — use the delete option available on each appointment, treatment, doctor, or reminder within the app.
  • Clear all app data — on Android, go to Settings → Apps → DentalHistory → Storage → Clear data. This permanently removes all records, settings, and cached files.
  • Uninstall the app — uninstalling DentalHistory from your device removes all locally stored data. Any backup files you exported previously are not affected and must be deleted separately.

Because the app is fully offline and the developer does not receive or store your data on any server, there is no remote copy to request deletion of.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or product changes. The latest version should always be published at the active policy URL.
